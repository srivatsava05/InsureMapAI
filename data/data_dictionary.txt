Synthetic training dictionary for legacy export columns (illustrative; not carrier documentation).
POL_NO: policy identifier assigned by the source policy system.
INS_NM: insured name stored as LAST, FIRST.
EFF_DT: policy effective date; mixed MMDDYYYY and YYMMDD strings.
EXP_DT: policy expiration date.
PREM_AMT: written premium stored as integer cents.
STAT_CD: A active, C cancelled, X expired, P pending; Z is a deliberate unknown.
LOB_CD: AUTO, HOME or PROP product family.
AGT_ID: producer identifier.
ST: two-character insured risk state.
ZIP5: primary five digit postal code.
ZIP4: optional postal suffix.
PHONE: insured telephone, mixed punctuation.
EMAIL: insured email address.
ADDR1: insured street address.
CITY: mailing city.
DEDUCT_AMT: deductible stored in cents.
LIMIT_AMT: limit stored in cents.
COV_CD: COLL collision, LIAB liability, COMP comprehensive.
VEH_YR: insured vehicle model year.
VIN: synthetic vehicle identifier.
RENEW_FLG: Y renewal, N new business.
CR_DT: source row creation date, ISO format.
CANCEL_DT: cancellation date, blank when not cancelled.
CLM_NO: claim number.
LOSS_DT: loss date; mixed YYYYMMDD and MM/DD/YYYY.
RPT_DT: date claim was reported, MMDDYYYY.
CLM_STAT: O open, C closed, R reopened.
RSV_AMT: current reserve in cents.
PAID_AMT: indemnity paid in cents.
